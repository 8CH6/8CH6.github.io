100 Likes

Grounded in Social Comparison Theory, this 90-second paper-cut collage animation explores how quantified social media metrics turn simple joy into self-doubt. Comparing her 100 likes to another’s 20,000, a girl layers viral outfits onto herself—growing increasingly restricted—before pinning her beloved orange-red fish brooch back on to reclaim her own sense of worth.
