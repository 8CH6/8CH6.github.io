Decided for You

Exploring automation bias and algorithmic authority, this absurdist urban short follows Lin Zhixia as she outsources everything from daily outfits to career and relationship choices to an AI assistant. When the system begins rewriting her messages and mediating her relationships, she finally turns off auto-decision to send an unpolished, authentic reply—reclaiming her right to doubt, err, and choose for herself.
