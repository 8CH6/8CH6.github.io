When a Helper Falls Ill, Who Pays?

Examining healthcare arrangements for foreign domestic helpers in Hong Kong, this journalistic feature explores the boundaries between employer medical liability, employees' compensation, and insurance coverage. Combining interviews with helpers and the public, a real-life case, and policy analysis of the Labour Department’s Standard Employment Contract, the piece highlights the perception gap between employers and helpers and outlines practical channels for seeking support.
