The Silent Bystander

Blending campus suspense with psychological reflection, this short film follows high schooler Shen Yan as he uncovers projector and phone clues revealing that his missing classmate’s cries for help were met with apathy—and his own silence. Confronted with the bystander effect, he turns in the evidence, owns up to his complicity, and reaches out to another struggling peer, reminding us that the real horror is not failing to see a cry for help, but choosing to look away.
